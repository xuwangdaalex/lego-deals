How this page works

This page reads BIG W products from the published LEGO Deals JSON. It does not scrape BIG W directly in your browser: BIG W's site restricts cross-site page access, and the GitHub Actions runner has recently timed out connecting to its catalogue. The BIG W catalogue link opens the retailer's live page separately.
